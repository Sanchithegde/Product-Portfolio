Product Case Studies

AI Spam Prevention for Telecom

This case study details the problem of high-volume spam calls for Jio users, analyzing its impact on customer experience and revenue. It proposes "Unnati Kavach," a network-level AI solution to detect and filter spam calls in real-time, aiming to reduce user frustration and improve call security.

Combating Misinformation on Social Media

This case study outlines a proposal to combat political misinformation on WhatsApp in India, the platform's largest market. It highlights the problem of image-based fake news influencing voter behavior and proposes an in-app, AI-powered system to detect and flag potential misinformation in real-time without compromising user privacy

Empowering India's Emerging Content Creators

This case study addresses the challenges faced by Indian content creators with fewer than 50,000 subscribers, particularly in content ideation, audience engagement, and monetization. It proposes "Tarang," an AI-powered SaaS platform designed to help creators discover content ideas and build loyal, monetizable communities.

B2B solution for brand protection

This case study examines the widespread issue of counterfeit products on Indian e-commerce platforms, highlighting their financial and reputational harm. It introduces PureMark, an AI-powered B2B SaaS platform designed to be a centralized verification system for brands, sellers, and products, ultimately creating a more trustworthy online marketplace.

Student Mental Health AI Platform

This case study addresses the severe academic stress and mental health issues among Indian students (ages 14-20) preparing for competitive exams. It proposes an AI platform, "S.A.T.H.I.," to offer proactive and personalized mental health support. A secondary solution for parents of young children (ages 0-5) is also mentioned to promote early emotional well-being.
